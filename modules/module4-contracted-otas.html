Part 1 · Levers & the rule

What contracted actually means

A contracted OTA is one on Dusit's approved list, with commissions and terms agreed centrally. You work inside those agreements. You do not renegotiate them at the hotel.

Promotions sit on top of the foundation. They never replace it. If the foundation is wrong, no promotion will fix it.

No price impact

The three safe levers

Ranking Booster

You pay the OTA, usually by performance, to rank higher.

+You only pay when it works
−Some quietly behave like a discount. Read what you sign.
Paid Advertising

You buy a top slot outright. Advertising, not a discount.

+Best ranking, zero ADR impact
−Needs a budget, and no guarantee of bookings
Value Added

Add a perk instead of cutting the rate. Upgrade, credit, breakfast.

+Maintains parity. The price never moves.
−Not every channel accepts it
The value an OTA guest receives must never beat what a Dusit Gold member gets. A perk can undercut your own programme just as a price can.
Price impact

The three that move the price

Member Exclusive

A deal shown only to the OTA's logged-in members.

+Your public price does not change
−Competes with Dusit Gold and shifts guests to their channel
Point of Sale

A discount aimed at one market only. China, the Middle East.

+Protects the public rate while targeting demand
−Every OTA manages POS differently, so it can leak
Package Promo

A lower rate hidden inside a bundle. Flight plus hotel, or a B2B plan.

+The low rate is fenced, and cancellations are low
−Can leak to B2B and metasearch. High cost.
Bundle and fence a price-impact lever before it goes on sale, never after.

Good place to pause. Next: the stacking rules, and how to choose.

CRITICAL Each OTA stacks promotions differently. Never assume.
EXPEDIA
No discount stacking without written approval from the Director of Distribution and OTA Partnerships.
The market manager will push you to stack. The answer is no, unless it is approved in writing.
AGODA
Opaque cannot be combined with Private Sales.
Two levers that look independent, but the platform treats them as one.
BOOKING
Take Genius Level 1 and stop. Never Level 2 or Level 3.
The higher tiers would put you below Dusit Gold on their own.
TRIP.COM
Clear endorsed promotions through the market manager first.
A conversation before activation, every time.
The safe default: when in doubt, do not stack. One lever chosen well almost always beats three piled on top of each other.
The decision

Choosing a lever, in order

1
Is the foundation right?
The fourteen rate plans, set up correctly. No promotion saves a broken base.
2
Can a green lever solve it?
A booster or ad to get seen. A value-added perk to compete without discounting.
3
Only if price must move, fence it
A targeted POS deal or a bundled package. Check that OTA's stacking rule before combining.
👑
Does it still leave Dusit Gold on top?
The final check, every time. If yes, go. If no, stop.
That ladder turns a pile of tempting levers into one clear decision.
